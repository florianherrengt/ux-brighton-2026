# UX Designer

**Location:** Remote (US or UK) · occasional travel to team offsites
**Seniority:** Mid-level (3–5 years of experience)

## About the role

We're looking for a UX Designer to join our product design team. You'll own design
for a product area end to end: from understanding user problems to delivering
polished, developer-ready solutions — and iterating on them once they're live.

## What you'll do day to day

- Design web and mobile product flows: wireframes, user flows, and hi-fi mockups in Figma
- Run and synthesize usability testing sessions and light user research
- Build interactive prototypes to communicate and test interaction ideas
- Contribute to and extend our design system and component library
- Partner daily with product managers and engineers; participate in design reviews
- Use analytics and user feedback to measure and improve your designs

## Must-have skills

- 3+ years designing digital products (web and mobile)
- Strong Figma skills (components, auto layout, prototyping)
- A portfolio showing real product work and your design process
- Experience with usability testing and translating findings into design decisions
- Comfortable working cross-functionally with PMs and engineers
- Clear written and verbal communication

## Nice-to-haves

- Experience maintaining or contributing to a design system
- Familiarity with accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Basic understanding of HTML/CSS
- Experience with A/B testing or experimentation

## Compensation

$90,000–$130,000 plus equity and benefits

## Company

A B2B SaaS company of 100–500 people with an established design team and a product-led
growth model.
